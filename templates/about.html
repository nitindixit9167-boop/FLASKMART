About FlaskMart

FlaskMart is a demo storefront built with Flask, SQLAlchemy, Flask-Login, and Flask-WTF on the backend, and Jinja2 with Bootstrap on the frontend. It's meant as a learning project and starting point — register an account, browse products and books, add items to your cart, place an order, and leave a review.
